About MercyMed

MercyMed Medical Center exists to improve the quality of life of our patients through competent, compassionate and comprehensive medical care, provided by locally trained professionals.

Our vision is to change the way healthcare is delivered in Kyrgyzstan through a holistic approach to body and spirit.
